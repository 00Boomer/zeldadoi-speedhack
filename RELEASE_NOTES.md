# ZeldaDOI Speedhack Edition

This patch brings Zelda: Dungeons of Infinity v1.1.6 to the **1 GB Anbernic
RG35XX SP running muOS**. Other Anbernic 1 GB devices are expected to work.

## Highlights

- Fast Lighting for smoother play in dim rooms, independent CRT settings,
  and 16:9, 3:2, 4:3, and 1:1 display options.
- Difficulty settings, including reduced damage and No Damage.
- Random and Dungeon Seed modes with SaveTent saves and fresh Continue.
  A successful Continue consumes the checkpoint, so save at another tent
  to create a new one.
- Selected v1.2.0 and v1.2.1 backports, including charged sword-spin
  improvements.

Install the original v1.1.6 PortMaster game first, then follow the
[installation guide](./README.md) to choose an installer ZIP. The patch
uses a separate folder with its own saves. Start with **Fast Lighting**
and **CRT Off** on the RG35XX SP.
